About My Work

My research focuses on understanding complexity in biological systems through computational models. I study how interactions among genes, proteins, cells, individuals, groups, and species affect their evolution, using approaches that allow us to analyze emergent properties of complex systems.

Some of my previous studies include analyzing human genetic variation data using genotype networks to identify convergent evolution, examining interactions between population size and mutation rate on empirical and RNA folding adaptive landscapes, and evaluating the role different potential causes of human dispersals from Africa.
