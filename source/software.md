# Software

I have created a few packages and frameworks:

* [__EvoDynamics.jl__](https://github.com/kavir1698/EvoDynamics.jl){:target="_blank"}: Simulation of eco-evolutionary dynamics on multi-trait networks.
* [__Agents.jl__](https://github.com/JuliaDynamics/Agents.jl){:target="_blank"}: An agent-based modeling framework.
* [__GraphNotes__](https://github.com/kavir1698/GraphNotes){:target="_blank"}: A tool that converts your research notes to a graph so that finding relevant information in the future becomes be easier.
* [__Moeen thesaurus__](https://github.com/kavir1698/Moeen_thesaurus){:target="_blank"}: a thesaurus of Persian nouns from Moeen Dictionary.
